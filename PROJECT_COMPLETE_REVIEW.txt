2. NON-NEGOTIABLE FINANCIAL INVARIANTS & CORE PRINCIPLES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Integer Paise Financial Representation:
   All monetary amounts in the database and intermediate arithmetic are stored and computed as BIGINT integer paise (1 Rupee = 100 Paise). Floating-point math is strictly prohibited for financial calculations to prevent binary rounding inaccuracies. Formatting to INR (₹) is performed purely at the presentation boundary using custom utility formatters.

2. Audited Double-Entry Ledger Discipline:
   Ledger balances are never modified client-side. Balances are derived idempotently from verified transaction rows, opening balances, and date-bounded adjustments.

3. Strict Non-Truncation Policy:
   Historical transactions and statements are never silently truncated (e.g., using naive .limit(N) queries). Safe batch-paginated fetching ensures datasets with thousands of transactions compute exact card and account balances.

4. Global Credit Card Limit Enforcement:
   Projected card outstanding (Opening Outstanding + Active Card Charges + Card-Linked EMI Principal - Repayments - Refunds - Credits) MUST NEVER exceed the card's configured credit limit. Enforcement is strictly dual-layered: instant real-time telemetry badge in the UI and hard rejection in server actions.

5. Strict Account Isolation:
   Income can ONLY be deposited into liquid bank accounts or physical cash wallets. Credit cards are strictly blocked from receiving income deposits.

6. Timezone & Date Discipline (IST):
   All transaction validation and financial schedules run strictly on Indian Standard Time (Asia/Kolkata / UTC+05:30). Future-dated transactions are strictly forbidden. Date boundary checks ensure transactions cannot precede an account's or card's opening date.

3. DETAILED MODULE-BY-MODULE IMPLEMENTATION REVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

MODULE A: AUTHENTICATION & MULTI-TENANT SECURITY
- Routes: /login, /auth/callback, /profile
- Technology: Supabase Auth with Server Component session extraction (createClient via @supabase/ssr).
- Multi-Tenant RLS: Every single database table implements PostgreSQL Row Level Security (auth.uid() = user_id) on SELECT, INSERT, UPDATE, and DELETE.
- User Display Resolution: Resolves email prefixes, metadata full names, or "Private Client" safely.

MODULE B: LIQUID ACCOUNTS & BALANCE COMPUTATION
- Routes: /accounts
- Capabilities:
  * Manages liquid bank accounts (Savings, Current) and physical cash wallets.
  * Captures initial opening balance and opening balance date.
  * Authoritative balance calculations: Opening Balance + Total Incomes - Total Debit Expenses - Transfers Out + Transfers In.
  * Real-time calculation of total liquid assets and liquid net worth.

MODULE C: UNIFIED SMART ONE-TAP TRANSACTION ENTRY
- Routes: /transactions, /transactions/new, /transactions/history
- Capabilities & 8-Tier UX Hierarchy:
  1. Income / Expense Dual Switch: Tactile toggle that contextually switches categories, placeholders, and allowed payment sources.
  2. Deterministic Category Ranking Engine: Pure scoring engine (quick-entry-engine.ts) ranking categories by decay-weighted recency and frequency. Strictly isolates income categories from expense categories and filters out debt repayments and transfers. Includes 1-tap category pinning.
  3. 1-Tap Transaction Templates & Routine Replay: User-scoped templates with custom names, pre-configured amounts, categories, and sources. Includes horizontal quick-tap carousel. Tapping pre-fills the form with the date strictly defaulted to TODAY (no stale dates). Guarantees zero automated posting without user verification.
  4. Fast Numeric Settlement Entry: Large prominent INR input with quick increment chips (+100, +500, +1,000, +2,000, +5,000), quick clear button, and live credit card limit telemetry badge.
  5. Contextual Merchant / Description: Smart placeholder hints and autofill based on selected source.
  6. Remembered Payment Sources: Independently remembers the user's preferred expense source (e.g., credit card) vs. income destination (bank account). Gracefully falls back if a source is archived.
  7. Date & Additional Memo: Defaults to IST Today; expandable metadata memo input.
  8. Verified Settlement Action: Double-submission protection (disables during transit with loading spinner) and hard credit limit lock.
  * Transaction Editing (/transactions/history): In-place editing of past transactions with automatic delta recalculations, date boundary checks, and balance integrity maintenance.

MODULE D: CREDIT CARDS & DEBT MANAGEMENT
- Routes: /cards, /cards/activity, /cards/statements
- Capabilities:
  * Card Registry: Bank name, custom card name, last 4 digits, cardholder name, billing cycle dates, credit limits, and opening outstanding balances.
  * Network Auto-Detection: Automatically detects and displays branded badges for Visa, Mastercard, RuPay, and American Express.
  * Global Limit Engine: Real-time calculation of credit limit, total utilized credit, available credit, and utilization percentage.
  * Card Activity Log: Non-truncating batch pagination across transactions, repayments, and refunds.
  * Statement Billing Schedule Engine: Computes statement generation date, due date, payment grace window, billing cycle status, and overdue alerts.

MODULE E: EMI & LOAN AMORTIZATION CENTER
- Routes: /emi, /emi/schedule, /emi/payments, /emi/edit, /emi/foreclosure, /emi/foreclosure/settle
- Capabilities:
  * Reducing Balance Amortization Engine: Calculates monthly EMIs, principal portions, interest portions, outstanding balance curves, and loan tenure.
  * Card-Linked EMI Accounting: Accurately reflects how card-converted EMIs ring-fence the card's credit limit while keeping amortized debt schedules separate from statement purchases.
  * Additional Loan Charges: Tracks processing fees, GST, bounce charges, and late fees.
  * Foreclosure Calculator & Multi-Quote Comparison: Simulates prepayment / early foreclosure, calculating foreclosure penalty fees, GST, future interest saved, and net cost-benefit analysis.
  * Foreclosure Settlement Workflow: Dedicated workflow to record lump-sum foreclosure payments, settle active debts, and instantly release credit card limit blocks.

MODULE F: RECURRING TRANSACTIONS & EXPECTED OCCURRENCES
- Routes: /transactions/recurring
- Capabilities:
  * Recurrence Rule Engine: Supports Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, and Yearly schedules with custom day-of-month and end-of-month handling.
  * Expected Items, Not Fake Transactions: Recurring rules do NOT create phantom completed transactions. They project expected upcoming dates and reminders.
  * Idempotent 1-Tap Confirmation: Users confirm expected occurrences as they actually happen, converting them cleanly into ledger transactions without duplicates.
  * Persistent Navigation: Dedicated navigation item with calendar/recurrence icon accessible on desktop, mobile bottom nav, and iOS PWA layouts.

MODULE G: MONTHLY CATEGORY BUDGETS & SPENDING ALERTS
- Routes: /budgets
- Capabilities:
  * Category Limits: Allows setting monthly spending caps by category, supporting default recurring monthly budgets and month-specific overrides (YYYY-MM).
  * Spending Telemetry: Real-time aggregation of all debit and credit card expenses against budgets for the active month.
  * Visual Progress & Status: Color-coded progress bars (Normal <50%, Approaching 50-79%, Critical 80-99%, Exceeded 100%+).
  * Multi-Tier Threshold Alerts: Configurable alerts at 50%, 80%, and 100% of budget.
  * Idempotent Alert Events: budget_alert_events table prevents duplicate alert notifications for the same threshold within the same month.
  * Dashboard Integration: Budget Health telemetry widget directly on the main dashboard.

MODULE H: DASHBOARD & TOTAL WEALTH TELEMETRY
- Routes: /
- Capabilities:
  * Consolidated Net Worth summary (Liquid Bank/Cash Assets minus Total Credit Card & EMI Liabilities).
  * Cashflow telemetry (Monthly Income vs. Monthly Expenses).
  * Upcoming Payments Center: Consolidates upcoming credit card statement dues, EMI instalments, and recurring bill payments into a single prioritized checklist.
  * Quick-action shortcut grid for recording transactions, adding accounts, reviewing cards, and managing EMIs.

MODULE I: PERFORMANCE OPTIMIZATIONS
- High-Efficiency Data Layer:
  * Migrated sequential waterfall database calls to concurrent Promise.all queries.
  * Added route loading skeletons (loading.tsx) for instant page transitions.
  * Memoized expensive amortization calculations to eliminate main-thread stuttering.
  * Added composite database indexes for user_id, transaction_date, and transaction_type.
